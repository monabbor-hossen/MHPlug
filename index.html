The Engine

Zero Global Bloat.

Most Elementor addons blindly inject massive CSS and JS files on every single page of your website, ruining your Google Lighthouse scores.

MH Plug utilizes Elementor's native dependency injection. Assets are registered, but they are never loaded unless that specific widget exists on the current page. The result? A pristine DOM and immediate load times.

Common Inquiries.

Everything you need to know about the MH System.

Does it require Elementor Pro?
No. MH Plug is designed to unlock Pro-level features—including Theme Building and Advanced WooCommerce—on the Free version of Elementor. You can save hundreds of dollars in annual subscriptions.
Will it slow down my website?
Absolutely not. Our core philosophy is "Zero Bloat." We use conditional asset enqueuing, meaning scripts and styles are only loaded when the specific widget is present on a page.
Is it compatible with my theme?
Yes. MH Plug is built following strict WordPress coding standards and Elementor's native API. It works seamlessly with Hello Elementor, Astra, GeneratePress, and any other standards-compliant theme.
